Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Overview. DailyBoard ("we", "this site") respects your privacy. This page explains what information is collected when you visit and how it is used.

Information we collect. With your permission, your browser's location is used to show local weather and local news. Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (weather) and BigDataCloud (to find your city name). We do not store your location on any server we control. If you deny permission, the site uses a default city instead. Your to-do items, theme choice, and cached rates and news are stored only in your own browser's local storage on your device; we do not receive or keep them.

Cookies and ads. This site may use third-party advertising vendors, including Google, to serve ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Third-party services. To show live content, this site contacts these services directly from your browser: Open-Meteo (weather and sunrise/sunset), BigDataCloud (location to city name), gold-api.com (gold, silver and platinum prices), Frankfurter (currency exchange rates), CoinGecko (Bitcoin and Ethereum prices), rss2json.com with Google News RSS (news headlines), and Google Fonts, plus the cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg content networks for scripts and icons. Google AdSense serves ads. Each of these services may log standard request details such as your IP address and browser type under its own privacy policy.

Your choices. You can disable location access anytime in your browser settings. You can also block cookies via your browser settings, though this may affect ad personalization.

Contact. Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.

Changes. This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
